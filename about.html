WELCOME TO BHARAT

An Indian-themed GTA San Andreas Multiplayer (SA-MP) roleplay project built around freedom, community and stories worth remembering.

OUR VISION

A city shaped by its citizens.

Bharat Roleplay is presented as an Indian-themed GTA San Andreas Multiplayer (SA-MP) roleplay world where players can develop careers, build relationships, join organizations and create their own stories. The best moments come from the choices people make together.

Whether you want to serve the public, run a business, explore the city or build a reputation, your journey is yours to shape.

THE EXPERIENCE

Freedom. Community. Progression.

Explore a world built around social interaction, player-led stories and a sense of progression. Server-specific features and availability should be confirmed with the project team.
