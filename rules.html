Stay In Character

React to situations as your character would. Keep out-of-character chat separate from roleplay.

Value Consent & Fair Play

Do not force outcomes on other players. Give others a reasonable chance to respond.

No Cheats or Exploits

Do not use cheats, unauthorized mods, bug abuse or duplicated money/items.

Avoid Random Violence

Do not attack or disrupt people without a valid roleplay reason under the server rules.

Respectful Communication

No harassment, hate, threats or sharing private information. Follow staff instructions and appeal calmly.

Report Problems Properly

Save relevant evidence and report incidents through the official support channel instead of retaliating.

Make your story count

Check official announcements for the current server address, commands, recruitment status and feature availability.
